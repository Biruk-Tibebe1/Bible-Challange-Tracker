# Challenge Days

The challenge runs for the complete Ethiopian calendar year 2019 E.C., from Meskerem 1 (Day 1) through Pagume 5 (Day 365), inclusive. Ethiopian dates are the primary challenge dates; Gregorian dates must not replace them in the main experience.

The Bible is the standard Protestant canon: 66 books and 1,189 chapters, from Genesis through Revelation. Every chapter must be assigned exactly once, with Revelation 22 as the final chapter on Day 365 / Pagume 5.

The eventual schedule must contain 271 days with 3 chapters and 94 days with 4 chapters (271 x 3 + 94 x 4 = 1,189). The schedule and chapter data have not been generated. See `challenge-config.ts` for the typed requirement constants.
